Prototype reference
Inspired by car #131: red roll hoop, blue suspension and bright aluminum bodywork. This is a simplified spatial concept, not chassis CAD.

Concept placement, not to scale. Proposed connections:

  • Front acquisition node to CM4 hub: 10BASE-T1S.
  • Rear acquisition node to CM4 hub: 10BASE-T1S, same multidrop trunk.
  • Haltech Elite 1500 ECU to CM4 hub: CAN, hardware receive-only.
  • GNSS antenna to ZED-F9P on the CM4 carrier: RF coax.
  • CM4 hub to Wi-Fi antenna: RF coax; then best-effort Wi-Fi telemetry to the pit server.
  • Pit server (InfluxDB and Grafana) to team laptop: dashboard client.
  • Vehicle PDM 12 V supply to the front node, CM4 hub and rear node.

The CM4 onboard log is authoritative; a Wi-Fi outage affects live viewing only. Use the component list and inspector for details of each part.

Accuracy notes

Hardware sizes, enclosure locations, harness lengths, and antenna mounting are illustrative pending packaging and vehicle measurements.
ZED-F9P and IMU are shown as functions on the CM4 carrier. GNSS and Wi-Fi antennas sit outside the enclosure via coax.
The Haltech CAN path is physically receive-only. Off-car Wi-Fi runs from CM4 to the pit server; Grafana is viewed on the team laptop.

Explore the FSAE DAQ

Use this self-contained 3D engineering concept to show how the central logger, remote acquisition nodes, engine CAN, antennas, and power distribution relate spatially.

  • Drag to orbit; Shift + drag or right-drag to pan.
  • Scroll / pinch to zoom; double-click the canvas to reset.
  • Click a 3D callout label or marker, or choose a component in the list.
  • Keys 1–4 switch modes, 0 fits the car, arrow keys advance the tour, Esc closes this help.
  • Explode lifts DAQ modules clear of the vehicle. X-Ray fades the seat, chassis and body to reveal the proposed DAQ hardware. System Only hides vehicle geometry entirely. Explode and visibility work independently.
  • Click the pit laptop or the Dashboard button to open an offline interactive Grafana-style telemetry preview.
  • Use Save image for a PNG suitable for a proposal or slide.
